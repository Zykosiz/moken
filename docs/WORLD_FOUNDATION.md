# Moken — World Foundation

## Core Premise

The world is an archipelago of islands separated by a supernatural darkness that has consumed much of the sea. Civilization survives, but it has become fragmented.

## The Sea

The darkness primarily inhabits the ocean rather than the islands themselves.

Crossing the sea is dangerous. The darkness distorts navigation, attracts monsters, and conceals the Horrors that sever routes between islands.

## Safe Islands

Most inhabited islands remain protected.

Life continues:
- Villages
- Harbors
- Farms
- Markets
- Taverns
- Festivals

The tragedy is not that these places are dead, but that many have become isolated from one another.

## Great Cities

Large cities have developed ways to resist the darkness and have become crowded refuges. They remain centers of trade, politics and culture, but suffer from overcrowding and dependence on reopened sea routes.

## Restoring the World

Defeating a Horror does not instantly heal the world.

Instead, it restores part of the surrounding sea, allowing ships, trade, travelers and communication to return. The player's journey gradually reconnects isolated communities.

## The Unknown

The origins of the darkness, the Horrors and the mysterious pistol are intentionally unknown at the beginning of the game. Discovery is a major part of the adventure.

## Canon Principles

- The world is wounded, not destroyed.
- Hope exists in every region.
- Every restored route has visible consequences.
- The sea is both the greatest danger and the greatest opportunity.
- Exploration should inspire curiosity as often as tension.
