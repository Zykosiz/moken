# Moken — Void Gun Canon

## Origin

The Void Gun originally belonged to the Pirate Captain who attacked Zeko's home island.

Before its transformation, it was already a famous and recognizable sidearm. It possesses a unique silhouette and ornate craftsmanship that experienced pirates and sailors can identify at a glance.

When the Cosmic Horror devoured the Captain, the pistol was fused with fragments of stolen starlight instead of being destroyed.

The resulting Void Gun retains enough of its original shape that those who once knew the Captain recognize it immediately.

---

## Narrative Purpose

The weapon carries two identities.

- The infamous sidearm of the Pirate Captain.
- The first weapon capable of restoring starlight to the universe.

Over the course of the story its meaning changes from a symbol of hatred into a symbol of hope.

---

## Future Crew Scene

At a later point in the journey, one of Zeko's future crewmates notices the gun instantly.

> "...Where did you get that gun?"

Zeko hesitates.

> "Is the one who carried it still alive?"

A long pause.

> "No."

Another pause.

> "If he was... I'd be able to kill him myself."

The crewmate quietly understands.

They also hated the Pirate Captain.

This becomes one of the first genuine moments of trust between them without requiring either character to explain their past.

---

## Design Direction

The Void Gun should always preserve the recognizable silhouette of the Captain's original weapon.

The Horror's corruption enhances rather than replaces it.

Visual language:

- blackened metal
- white starlight veins beneath the surface
- carved, organic ornamentation
- elegant but predatory silhouette
- unmistakably unique profile
